# Kinetic Gain Embedded

Board Committee Throughput Scorecard is designed to open an embedded executive-intelligence engagement:

- convert agenda overflow, slot scarcity, and committee carryover into explicit board-visible scheduling tradeoffs
- expose where added meetings or reordered packets can recover decision speed before another lane is funded
- tie escalation turnover and board confidence to named operating surfaces instead of abstract process complaints
- convert scattered committee bottlenecks into one reusable intervention packet for boards and investors
